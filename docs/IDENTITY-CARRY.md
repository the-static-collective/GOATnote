# IC-001 · Identity Carry

**Status: proposed writing method, not a diagnosis, test of personhood, or mandatory template.**

Writing can help a person maintain contact with their history, develop new understanding and capacities, protect the distinction between their account and others' descriptions, and carry forward what they choose without erasing earlier experience. The writing is a carrier of identity, not the entirety of identity.

## Six optional moves

1. **Occurrence:** What happened or is happening? Preserve concrete particulars and what you do not know.
2. **Witness:** What did you notice, experience, feel, think, or do? Distinguish experience from explanations about other people.
3. **Discernment:** What is observed, remembered, reported, inferred, disputed, or still unresolved?
4. **Agency:** What remains yours to choose, refuse, request, protect, or leave undecided? Do not manufacture control where none exists.
5. **Carry:** What deserves to remain available to a future you—fact, memory, commitment, boundary, question, poem, or correction?
6. **Re-entry:** At a later time, read voluntarily. What is recognizable, revised, newly understood, or still unknown? Add a margin or new entry; do not overwrite an earlier record to make it conform.

Free writing may come before, after, between, or instead of these prompts. No entry is required to produce a moral, positive feeling, coherent narrative, or permanent self-definition.

## Two working modes

**Free page:** Write naturally, including music, anger, humor, prayer, fragment, interruption, or silence. Creative language need not be literal testimony.

**Witness margin:** Attach a later reading beside a particular passage, tagged as witness / question / interpretation / correction / carry / connection. Keep original language and version identity unchanged. Allow incompatible readings to coexist as attributable readings without declaring them all equally evidenced.

## Invariants

- An earlier account remains attributable even if the author later changes their mind.
- No single entry is an exhaustive identity verdict.
- Uncertainty, contradiction, privacy, unfinished thought, and not rereading are legitimate states.
- Source ≠ interpretation ≠ established external event.
- A prior experience does not oblige disclosure, public performance, or further processing.
- An AI reader, if added, assists the writer and does not own, diagnose, authenticate, or define their identity.

## Tiny example

Original: “I cooked breakfast and couldn't remember the last quiet morning.”

Later margin (witness): “That ordinary morning was important to me.”

Later margin (question): “What made it feel different?”

Later margin (carry): “Keep the recipe and the memory; do not decide what the whole season meant today.”

All four remain distinct records.
